## New
- **Timecode countdown:** Settings → Timecode reads incoming timecode (LTC) from a sound card input, such as a Dante Virtual Soundcard channel. Add a timer for a stretch of timecode and the point where the countdown should reach zero, or press the target buttons to take the times from the live signal. While the timecode is in that stretch, the playback countdown counts down to zero, taking over from QLab or Mitti, and hands back once the timecode passes zero or stops. The desktop app reads the timecode in the background; on another machine, open /timecode-reader in a browser there. The first time you turn it on, macOS asks to allow Ontime to use the microphone: allow it, as that is how it hears the timecode input
