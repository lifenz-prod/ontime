## Fixed
- **Backstage notes:** the Now, Next and Then cards show each event's notes again. They're on by default; to hide them, set Event secondary text to None in the view settings
- **Backstage schedule:** the schedule down the right no longer flips through pages every 10 seconds. It stays on the upcoming events unless you turn cycling back on in the view settings
- **View settings:** Save in the Backstage settings now keeps your choices. Before, choosing Note or Stop cycling did nothing, and Stop cycling couldn't be turned off
