## New
- **Re-order timecode timers:** in Settings → Timecode, each timer has up and down arrows to move it in the list. Order matters: where two timers overlap, the one higher in the list runs the countdown

## Changed
- **Timecode Save button:** Save and Revert to saved are now at the bottom right of the timer list, on the same line as Add timer, instead of at the top of the panel
