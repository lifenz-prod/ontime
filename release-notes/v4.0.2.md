## New
- **Quick Start guide:** the guides for volunteers and production staff are now in the app. Open **Quick Start** at the top of the menu, or download them as a PDF
- **One-click updates:** About → Updates checks GitHub for a new version and shows its release notes. In the desktop app, **Update and restart** installs it. Opt in to beta releases there if you want test builds
- **PREV button:** goes back to the previous cue and starts it, for when someone advances too far. It replaces the skip buttons on every editor, and the Play button on the iPad Editor
- **Google Sheet import without signing in:** paste the link to a sheet shared as "Anyone with the link can view". The last five sheets are one tap away, and the **Currently loaded sheet** box switches tabs without re-pasting the link
- **Ontime Cue Renderer:** a Mac app that sends Ontime's cue screens to ProPresenter as a Syphon video source, for when its web view flickers. It's attached to this release
- **Mobile cue screen,** and an orange **AUX** tag on cues shown as the aux timer
- **Mitti:** the playback countdown can follow Mitti as well as QLab

## Changed
- The compact editor is now the everyday **Editor**; the full one is the **Advanced Editor**
- The iPad Editor's controls are sized for touch, and its left column scrolls so every cue option is in reach
- Adding a cue on the iPad Editor and the Editor offers just **+ Event**
- The end-action icon only shows for Stop, Play next and Play next after delay
- The settings menu shows just its headings, and "Data Sources" is now **Import**
- The menu no longer lists Minimal Timer, Operator, Cuesheet, Wall Clock, Timeline, Public, Studio Clock, Lower Thirds, Countdown or Project Info
- Edits to the 11am service stick, and the service tabs are simpler

## Fixed
- A cue screen that reloads during a network blip comes back from cache instead of going blank
- A partial save no longer switches the playback countdown back to QLab
- Panels' close buttons no longer cover their save buttons
- macOS builds no longer fail when Apple's servers drop the connection
