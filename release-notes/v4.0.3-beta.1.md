## New
- **Ableton countdown:** Settings → Ableton follows Session View clips in Ableton Live through AbletonOSC. Add a timer for a clip and the time in it where the countdown should reach zero, or press the target button to take the time from Live's playhead. While that clip plays, the playback countdown counts down to that point, taking over from QLab or Mitti, and hands back once the clip passes it or stops. Scrubbing the clip moves the countdown with it

## Changed
- The Playback Countdown view is now called **Video Countdown**. Its address is unchanged, so ProPresenter layers pointing at it keep working
